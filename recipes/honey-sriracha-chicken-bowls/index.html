Honey Sriracha Chicken Bowls

Sticky honey sriracha chicken with jasmine rice, broccoli, and fresh avocado.

Let’s make it

  1. Rinse the jasmine rice. Cook with the water and a pinch of salt according to the package directions. The supplied method uses a covered, low simmer for 15 minutes followed by 10 minutes resting off the heat; adjust water and timing to your rice brand.
  2. Cut the chicken into 1-inch pieces and pat dry. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  3. Whisk the honey, sriracha, soy sauce or coconut aminos, and optional ginger in a small bowl.
  4. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer, working in batches if necessary. Cook for about 6–8 minutes, turning, until browned and the center reaches 165°F.
  5. Lower the heat to medium, add the sauce, and toss for 1–2 minutes until the chicken is coated and the sauce thickens. Watch closely so the honey does not burn.
  6. Cook the frozen broccoli according to its package directions. A microwave-safe bowl with a vented cover typically takes about 5–6 minutes, depending on the microwave.
  7. Divide the chicken, rice, and broccoli into four bowls using either equal portions or the portion guide below. Add freshly sliced avocado when serving.

Save some for later

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours in shallow containers. Reheat until the center reaches 165°F; microwave time varies by portion and appliance. Add avocado fresh when eating.

Soy sauce typically contains soy and wheat; check product labels if using coconut aminos or making other substitutions.
