Chocolate Chip Peanut Butter Protein Cookies

Simple ingredients, one bowl, and a little chocolate on top.

Yield10–12 cookies
Bake time15 minutes
Oven375°F

Ingredients

Tap an ingredient to check it off.

Let’s make them

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Mash the bananas in a mixing bowl.
  3. Mix in the tahini, powdered peanut butter, and Vanilla Bean protein powder, adding and mixing each ingredient one at a time.
  4. If you like chocolate chips inside your cookies, mix in your preferred amount.
  5. Scoop into 10–12 cookies on the lined baking sheet. Flatten each cookie before baking.
  6. Sprinkle about ¼ cup chocolate chips across the tops of the cookies.
  7. Bake for 15 minutes.
